# STREETLAYER — Mumbai Field Research Kit

Goal: talk to 10–20 vendors, learn what they'd actually use. Research, not selling. Get consent before photos/recording.

## Opening (Hindi)
"Namaste! Hum ek chhota app bana rahe hain jisse aas-paas ke customers aapki dukaan map par dekh sakein. 5 minute baat kar sakte hain?"

## Questions
1. Customers aap ko kaise payment karte hain? (cash / UPI / other)
2. Naya maal (fresh stock) kab aata hai? Customers ko kaise batate ho?
3. Regular customers kitne hain? Unhe wapas bulane ke liye kya karte ho?
4. Kabhi crypto ke baare mein suna hai? Koi customer ne crypto se pay karna chaha?
5. Agar payment simple ho aur naye customers milein, kya try karoge?
6. Phone pe photo/voice se maal list karna aasaan lagega ya type karna?
7. Kaunsa feature aap roz use karoge?

## Record (per vendor)
| Market | Stall type | Payment today | Stock day | Uses smartphone? | Interest 1–5 | Quote | Consent for demo listing? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Do / Don't
- ✅ Say it's a hackathon prototype using test money
- ✅ Ask permission before using their stall name
- ❌ Don't promise customers, money or tax benefits
- ❌ Don't photograph people without consent
- ❌ Don't ask for bank/UPI details
